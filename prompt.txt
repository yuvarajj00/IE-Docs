Build the interface in a warm editorial / refined-minimal aesthetic — the opposite of generic SaaS. Use these exact rules:
Typography: Pair Fraunces (serif, weights 500–700) for all headings, numbers, balances, and buttons with Spline Sans Mono (monospace, 400–600) for body text, labels, and inputs. Headings use tight letter-spacing (-0.02em); uppercase micro-labels use wide tracking (0.15em) at ~0.64rem in a muted ink color.
Color — light mode: warm paper background #f4f1ea, card surface #fffdf8, inset fields #efeadf, near-black ink #1c1a17, muted ink #6b655c, hairline borders #e0d9cb. Accent is a terracotta red #c0492b (soft tint #f2dcd4); secondary semantic colors are forest green #3f6b52 for income/positive and slate blue #355b8c for neutral actions.
Color — dark mode: background #15140f, card #1f1d17, inset #28251d, ink #f1ede2, borders #322e25, accent brightens to #e8704f, green to #7bb392. Implement via data-theme attribute on <html> and CSS custom properties; default to system preference and provide a circular toggle button that rotates slightly on hover.
Surfaces & spacing: generous rounded corners (16px cards, 10–12px inputs/buttons), soft layered shadows (0 1px 2px + 0 8px 24px at low opacity), a subtle radial accent glow in the top-left of the page background. One "hero" stat card inverts colors (dark fill on light theme) to anchor the eye.
Motion: restrained and purposeful — 0.4s theme cross-fade, staggered fade-up on panel/list entry, gentle hover lift on cards (translateY(-3px)), 0.6s ease on progress-bar fills. No gratuitous micro-animations.
Layout: max-width ~1000px centered, pill-shaped tab navigation, two-column form grids that collapse to a single column under 720px, toast notifications that slide up from bottom-center (red variant for errors).
Avoid: Inter/Roboto/Arial, purple gradients, flat material-style cards, and evenly-distributed timid palettes. Commit to the terracotta-on-paper identity with dominant warm neutrals and sharp accent punctuation.
